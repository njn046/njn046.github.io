Privacy Policy

Үг холбоё: Mongolian Words · Last updated 20 November 2026

Үг холбоё ("the app") is made by Ninjin Jigmedochir, an individual developer. This policy explains what data the app uses.

What the app keeps on your phone

Your level progress, coins, found words and settings (language, sound, vibration) are saved only on your device. There are no accounts and we do not receive this data. Deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app can show optional rewarded video ads, served by Google AdMob, when you choose to watch one for a hint. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. On iPhone, the advertising identifier is only used if you allow tracking in Apple's prompt. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice later in the app's settings. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, we do not collect your name, email or contacts, and the app is not directed at children under 13.
